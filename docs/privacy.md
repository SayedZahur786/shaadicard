# Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

## Who operates this service
ShaadiCard is an early-preview project maintained by the repository owner, SayedZahur786. For privacy, deletion or service questions, use the project’s GitHub issue tracker at https://github.com/SayedZahur786/shaadicard/issues. Do not post guest names, passwords or other private information publicly. Use the invitation dashboard to delete invitations and replies directly. A dedicated private support channel and merchant identity must be established before paid launch.

## Information and purpose
We store the account email address, a salted password hash, session records, invitation names and venues, ceremony dates, optional shop branding, and family RSVP names and headcounts. Account information protects access to your private dashboard. Invitation details are public to anyone who has the link; do not add sensitive information. RSVP details are visible to the invitation owner. We do not request phone numbers, addresses, card details or photographs.

## Cookies and analytics
Essential HTTP-only cookies keep hosts signed in for up to 14 days and let guests revise their replies from the same browser for up to 180 days. They are not advertising cookies. The shared analytics component is disabled; no analytics events, tracking pixels or advertising SDKs are enabled. We hash a network identifier into a short-lived database rate-limit bucket to limit abuse; we do not use it for profiling.

## Service providers and transfers
Neon provides PostgreSQL storage, with this project configured in Singapore. Vercel hosts the application and may process requests and operational logs in other regions. GitHub hosts source code and public issue reports. Clicking Share on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with the invitation link; WhatsApp then applies its own terms. We do not automatically send messages or share RSVP lists with WhatsApp.

## Retention and security
Invitation and RSVP records remain until the owner deletes the invite; deletion cascades to its responses. Accounts remain until account deletion is requested or performed from the dashboard. Expired sessions and rate-limit buckets are removed by a maintenance command; backups may retain deleted data according to the provider’s configured recovery window. We use encrypted transport, password hashing, owner authorization and request validation. No service can guarantee absolute security. Do not reuse passwords.

## Your choices and rights
Hosts can view and delete their invitations and all associated replies in the dashboard. Guests can update their answer from the same browser, including declining every ceremony; for deletion of a reply, contact the host. Account holders may request account deletion through the contact route above without disclosing private data. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction or erasure. No claim of legal certification is made.

## Changes
We will update this page and its effective date when processing changes. Review it before creating a new invitation. Paid service, analytics or new data collection will require updated disclosures.
